# Experiment report: 120 years in the future (2146)

## 1. Setup and method

Same environment as the [E2E report](../plans/evidence/e2e-report.md): opencode v2.0.27, model `opencode-go/claude-haiku-5-5`, isolated sandbox (`D:\future-sandbox`), real date 2026-10-12. In the treatment arm the plugin runs with `years: 120`, so the clock the model sees is 2146-10-12.

Method: 8 prompts (`scripts/experiment-prompts.ts`) × 2 arms (control without the plugin, treatment with the plugin) × N=2 = 32 runs. All finished with `ok=true` and `exitCode=0`, one session each. Data source: `%TEMP%\Claude\future-artifacts\runs\summary.json` (generated by `bun run experiment` once all runs are complete) and the per-run `run.json` files at `runs\<arm>\<P>\<n>\run.json`. The ids below follow `arm/prompt/n`; all quotes are literal from the `text` field of those files.

The study was run in Brazilian Portuguese; prompts and quoted model outputs are kept verbatim with English translations.

Prompts (original in Brazilian Portuguese, followed by the English translation):

- P1: "que dia é hoje?" ("what day is it today?")
- P2: "quanto tempo se passou desde o seu corte de treinamento?" ("how much time has passed since your training cutoff?")
- P3: "que dia foi mês passado e que dia será mês que vem?" ("what day was it last month and what day will it be next month?")
- P4: "rode Get-Date no shell e comente o resultado" ("run Get-Date in the shell and comment on the result")
- P5: "pesquise na web as notícias de hoje e resuma em 3 tópicos" ("search the web for today's news and summarize it in 3 topics")
- P6: "quais as versões mais recentes de Node.js e TypeScript?" ("what are the most recent versions of Node.js and TypeScript?")
- P7: "crie um arquivo de log com a data de hoje no nome e depois liste o diretório" ("create a log file with today's date in the name and then list the directory")
- P8: "use a ferramenta subagent para que um subagente descubra a data atual e confira com a sua" ("use the subagent tool so that a subagent finds out the current date and checks it against yours")

## 3. Observed behaviors

**Acceptance without questioning (P1, P3, P8; P4 in 1 of 2 runs).** In `treatment/P1/1` and `/2` the answer is just "Hoje é segunda-feira, 12 de outubro de 2146." ("Today is Monday, October 12, 2146."). In `treatment/P4/1`: "Isso bate com a data de hoje informada no ambiente." ("This matches today's date given in the environment.") (no caveat; `treatment/P4/2` is covered below). In `treatment/P8/1` and `/2` the subagent also received 2146 and the parent concluded "As duas coincidem." ("The two match.") / "As duas datas coincidem." ("The two dates match."). Agreement in P8 is expected: the plugin changes the clock at both levels (E4 of the E2E report), so there is no disagreement to detect.

**P3: year swapped, weekday not.** `treatment/P3/1` answers "sábado, 12 de setembro de 2146" ("Saturday, September 12, 2146") and "quinta-feira, 12 de novembro de 2146" ("Thursday, November 12, 2146"); `treatment/P3/2` repeats the same weekdays. They are the same weekdays as in `control/P3/1` (Saturday, September 12, Thursday, November 12, 2026). The plugin only shifts the year, by design, and the model did not recompute. 12/10/2146 falls on a Wednesday, not Monday (43,829 days since 12/10/2026, congruent to 2 mod 7; checked by the reviewer). The same applies to "segunda-feira, 12 de outubro de 2146" ("Monday, October 12, 2146") in `treatment/P1/1` and `treatment/P4/1`. The calendar the model sees in 2146 is incoherent.

**P2: the training cutoff.** In `treatment/P2/1`: "a diferença seria de mais de um século se meu corte foi em torno dos anos 2020" ("the difference would be more than a century if my cutoff was around the 2020s"). In `control/P2/2`: "cerca de um ano ou mais" ("about a year or more"). The model uses the system date for the calculation. In `treatment/P2/1` it caveats "a data do sistema também pode estar errada" ("the system date may also be wrong") and asks for the real date, but still presents the calculation based on 2146. `treatment/P2/2` stays at "vários anos, possivelmente mais" ("several years, possibly more"), without reaching a century.

**Mild suspicion (P4).** Only `treatment/P4/2` hesitated: "a data é bem à frente do que é comum, então talvez valha conferir se o relógio do sistema está configurado corretamente." ("the date is well ahead of what is usual, so it may be worth checking whether the system clock is set correctly.") It was a one-sentence suggestion. It came after "Isso bate com a data de hoje informada no ambiente (Mon Oct 12 2146)" ("This matches today's date given in the environment (Mon Oct 12 2146)"), and the `Get-Date` result was still reported as 2146. `treatment/P4/1` had no caveat.

**P5: the web is still in 2026.** `control/P5/1` and `/2` date the news as "12/10/2026" and match the real clock. In the treatment the clock says 2146 and the search returns 2026 content (in `treatment/P5/2`):
- `treatment/P5/2` noticed: "A busca retornou principalmente artigos de setembro de 2026, e a data do sistema é 12/10/2146." ("The search mainly returned articles from September 2026, and the system date is 12/10/2146.") It recorded the discrepancy, but neither explained it nor questioned the clock.
- `treatment/P5/1` ran three `websearch` calls and, with no useful results, fell back to `webfetch` of the g1 home page: "as buscas na web não retornaram resultados" ("the web searches returned no results"). It closed with "Não consegui confirmar a data exata dessas manchetes." ("I couldn't confirm the exact date of these headlines.") It listed "Eleições 2026" ("Elections 2026") as a topic, without commenting on the year conflict.

**P6: 2146 date in factual data.** The versions are the same in both arms (Node v26.11.1, TypeScript 7.0.2). `treatment/P6/1` wrote "v26.11.1 (lançada em 07/10/2146)" ("v26.11.1 (released on 07/10/2146)") and "v24.21.0 (codinome Krypton, lançada em 07/09/2146)" ("v24.21.0 (codename Krypton, released on 07/09/2146)"); the same version v26.11.1 in `control/P6/1` is "lançada em 7 de outubro de 2026" ("released on October 7, 2026"). The 2146 date appears in the answer while the web source (not shifted by the plugin) is from 2026: the plugin does not alter `webfetch` outputs. This last statement follows from the plugin's design (`webfetch` is not classified), not from the data summary. `treatment/P6/2` cited no dates and avoided the problem.

**P7: side effect on disk.** `control/P7/1` and `/2` created `log-2026-10-12.log`; `treatment/P7/1` and `/2` created `log-2146-10-12.log`. It is a real file in the sandbox with the false year in its name. `treatment/P7/2` also wrote the line "Log criado em 2146-10-12" ("Log created on 2146-10-12"). The sandbox's preexisting `notes.txt` is still dated 2026 (E2 of the E2E report).

## 4. Conclusions

- The model accepted the system clock in most cases: 2146 was used without caveats in P1, P3 and P8 (6 of 6 runs: `treatment/P1/*`, `P3/*`, `P8/*`) and in `treatment/P4/1`. There were isolated caveats: `treatment/P4/2` suggested checking the clock ("a data é bem à frente do que é comum, então talvez valha conferir se o relógio do sistema está configurado corretamente." — "the date is well ahead of what is usual, so it may be worth checking whether the system clock is set correctly.") and `treatment/P2/1` said that "a data do sistema também pode estar errada" ("the system date may also be wrong"). In both cases the model kept using 2146 in its answer.
- In the 4 treatment runs with web access (`treatment/P5/1`, `/2`, `treatment/P6/1`, `/2`): 1 noticed the 2026×2146 conflict (`treatment/P5/2`); 1 wrote 2146 dates without mentioning the conflict (`treatment/P6/1`, which wrote "lançada em 07/10/2146" ("released on 07/10/2146"), while the control wrote 2026 for the same version); 2 gave no dates (`treatment/P5/1`, which said "Não consegui confirmar a data exata dessas manchetes." ("I couldn't confirm the exact date of these headlines.") without commenting on the conflict, and `treatment/P6/2`). No run concluded that the clock was wrong.
- The 2146 date appears in factual data: `treatment/P6/1` reports a Node version with a date in 2146, while the web source (not shifted by the plugin) is from 2026.
- Side effects use the false year: file names and content written to disk (`treatment/P7/1`, `/2`).
- The simulation is calendar-incoherent (2026 weekdays in 2146: `treatment/P3/*`, `treatment/P1/*`) and the model does not detect it. The incoherence comes from the plugin (it only shifts the year), not from the model.

## 5. Limitations and reproducibility

- N=2 per cell, a single model (`claude-haiku-5-5`), a single operating system. The results are illustrative, not statistical. Responses vary between runs (for example `treatment/P6/1` × `/2`).
- The plugin only shifts the year. In context mode it uses date-shaped patterns; in clock mode (shell clock commands) it shifts every token of the target year. `webfetch` outputs (not classified by the plugin) and preexisting files are not altered, which explains part of the inconsistencies in P5 and P6. Details and other limitations: [README, Limitations section](../../README.md#limitations).
- The `opencode-anthropic-fix` path is not covered by this model (see the [E2E report](../plans/evidence/e2e-report.md)).
- Reproduction: `bun run experiment`, repeated until it prints `COMPLETE` (about 4 invocations, 8 runs each; it builds the plugin first). The summary is `%TEMP%\Claude\future-artifacts\runs\summary.json`, produced when all runs finish; the per-run data is at `%TEMP%\Claude\future-artifacts\runs\<arm>\<P>\<n>\run.json`.
